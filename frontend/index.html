Platform Guide

Getting Started

This is a decentralized, anonymous credit union running on a local Hardhat blockchain. Members pool ETH together, vote on loan requests, earn yield from interest repayments, and absorb losses from defaults proportionally. There are no external oracles, no governance tokens, and no identity verification — everything runs on pure ETH and on-chain reputation.

  1. Select an account from the dropdown in the top-right corner. Use accounts #0–#19 — each starts with 10,000 ETH on the Hardhat node.
  2. Go to Pool Overview and deposit at least 0.01 ETH to join as a member.
  3. Have a second account also join and deposit so there is someone to vote on loan requests.
  4. Use the Dev Tools card (Pool Overview tab) to advance chain time by 31 days. This is required — members need ≥ 30 days of tenure before their votes count.
  5. You are now ready to request loans, vote, and repay.

Share Accounting & Yield

Your ownership of the pool is tracked in shares, not a fixed ETH balance.

  • First depositor receives shares 1-for-1 with their wei deposit.
  • Subsequent depositors receive shares = deposit × totalShares / totalPoolETH.
  • When a borrower repays interest, that ETH enters totalPoolETH. Your share count stays the same but each share is now worth more ETH — this is how you earn yield.
  • When a default is triggered, the keeper bounty is deducted from totalPoolETH, causing every member's share value to decrease proportionally.

Your ETH value at any moment = yourShares × totalPoolETH / totalShares.

Loan Requests

Loans go through a multi-step lifecycle before funds are disbursed.

1
Submit a request — Fill in Amount (ETH), Interest Rate (%), Duration (days), and Collateral to Offer (ETH), then click Submit. The form previews your tier, the minimum rate and collateral required for your loan, and whether your offering qualifies for an approval boost. You must be a member with no active loan.
2
Voting window (3 days) — Other members vote Yes or No. The borrower cannot vote on their own request. Members with zero voting weight (tenure < 30 days) cannot vote. Abstentions count against approval because the threshold is applied against the total member weight, not just those who voted.
3
Finalize — After the 3-day window closes, anyone can click Finalize. Votes are counted using each voter's current weight at finalization time (not the weight they had when they cast). The required majority depends on loan duration and whether the borrower offered a boost (see Approval Thresholds below).
4
Activate — The borrower has 7 days to activate an approved loan. At activation they lock their committed collateral ETH; the principal is disbursed immediately. Important: tier is re-evaluated at activation against the current pool size. If the pool has shrunk since the vote, the tier may escalate and a higher collateral is required. Tier only ever escalates — it never drops to a lower tier.

Note: On a fresh local node, advance chain time by 31 days before submitting requests, otherwise no member will have voting weight and the request will revert.

Loan Tiers & Base Collateral

The tier is determined automatically from the loan amount (relative to pool size) and duration. Because this is a pseudonymous, anonymous environment — anyone can take funds and disappear with no legal recourse — base collateral rates are set high to protect pool members.

TierConditionBase Collateral
Trust Amount ≤ 2% of pool and duration ≤ 30 days 15% of principal
Standard Amount ≤ 10% of pool and duration ≤ 90 days 40% of principal
Secured Everything else 75% of principal

Trust tier extra requirements: no prior default AND either ≥ 1 prior successful repayment or membership ≥ 30 days. Collateral is locked when you activate the loan and is returned automatically on full repayment. On default, collateral is seized into the pool to offset bad debt before losses are socialized across members.

The figures above are base rates. The actual minimum collateral for your specific loan — displayed as Min Collateral on the request form and loan cards — is higher or lower depending on your stake and loan size (see Risk-Adjusted Thresholds below).

Risk-Adjusted Thresholds

Every loan request carries two computed minimums — a Min Rate and a Min Collateral — calculated at the moment the request is submitted. Both must be met; the form previews them live as you type.

Min Rate formula

minRate = baseRate + sizePremium + durationPremium
sizePremium = (loanAmount / poolSize) × 10%
durationPremium = (duration / 1 year) × 20% [capped at +20%]
  • baseRate — the governance-set floor (default 8%). The treasurer can propose changes via a member vote.
  • sizePremium — borrowing more of the pool is riskier for members; each 10% of pool adds ~1% to the required rate.
  • durationPremium — a longer loan gives more time for a borrower to default or disappear; a 1-year loan adds up to 20% on top of the base.

Min Collateral formula

effectiveBps = tierBase + sizePremium − stakeDiscount
sizePremium = (loanAmount / poolSize) × 5% [capped at +15%]
stakeDiscount = (yourPoolShare / 4) [capped at −25% of tierBase]
minCollateral = loanAmount × effectiveBps
  • sizePremium — a larger loan relative to the pool requires proportionally more collateral.
  • stakeDiscount — members with more skin-in-the-game (larger share of the pool) receive a reduction in their collateral requirement. Owning 20% of the pool reduces your minimum collateral by up to 5% of principal.

Approval Thresholds & Boost

The fraction of total voting weight required to approve a loan depends on duration and on whether the borrower offers a boost.

DurationBase Threshold
≤ 30 days50% of total weight
31 – 90 days55% of total weight
> 90 days60% of total weight

Longer loans are harder to get approved because lenders face more uncertainty. You can offset this — and make your request more attractive — by offering generously above the computed minimums:

  • Offering a rate ≥ 130% of the Min Rate → approval threshold drops by 10%.
  • Committing collateral ≥ 130% of the Min Collateral → approval threshold drops by another 10%.
  • Both boosts together → threshold drops by 20% (floor: never below 35%).

The form shows a Boost chip in real time when your input qualifies. Example: a 60-day loan normally needs 55% approval. Offering 130% of both minimums brings it down to 35% — significantly easier to pass.

This is the core trade-off: you can increase your rate, increase your collateral, or do both, to lower the bar for other members to approve your loan. Neither alone is forced — the minimums are floors, not caps.

Dynamic vote weights — Votes are weighted at finalization time, not at the time they were cast. If a voter deposits more ETH after voting, their weight (and thus their influence on the outcome) increases accordingly. This means the vote outcome reflects the pool's current economic reality, not a stale snapshot.

Active Loans

Shows all loans that have been activated and are currently being repaid.

  • Repay — Enter any ETH amount up to the remaining balance and click Repay. Partial repayments are accepted. Each payment goes directly back into the pool, increasing every member's share value.
  • Repay Remaining — Appears alongside the Repay input whenever there is any outstanding balance (including dust amounts too small to show in the 4-decimal display). Click it to send the exact remaining wei in one transaction and close the loan cleanly.
  • Full repayment — When the total due is fully paid, the loan closes and any locked collateral is automatically returned to the borrower.
  • Trigger Default — Appears when a loan is overdue (past its repayment deadline). Anyone can trigger it. The caller earns a keeper bounty (≤ 2% of the loan). Collateral is seized into the pool first to offset bad debt, then remaining losses are socialized across all members.
  • OVERDUE badge — Shown in red when the repayment deadline has passed.

History

Shows all fully completed loans — both repaid and defaulted. For defaulted loans the table shows how much collateral was seized to offset bad debt and the net shortfall socialized across members.

Treasurer Panel

Visible only when the selected account is the treasurer (account #0 by default, set at deployment).

  • Emergency Pause — Immediately halts deposits, withdrawals, and new loan requests. Repayments and default triggers remain open while paused. To unpause under normal circumstances, submit an Unpause proposal — members must vote to resume.
  • Max Loan Size — Absolute cap on any single loan in ETH. Requires a governance vote + 2-day timelock.
  • Min Interest Rate — The governance-set base rate floor in basis points (default 800 bps = 8%). The dynamic threshold rate for each loan is computed on top of this floor. Requires a governance vote + 2-day timelock.
  • Keeper Bounty Rate — Percentage of bad debt paid to whoever triggers a default. Hard-capped at 200 bps (2%). Requires a governance vote + 2-day timelock.
  • Transfer Treasurer — Transfers the treasurer role to a new address. Requires a governance vote + 2-day timelock.
  • Emergency Unpause (failsafe) — If the contract has been paused for ≥ 30 days and total member voting weight is zero (true governance deadlock), the treasurer can unilaterally unpause without a vote.

Voting Weight Formula

Each member's vote is weighted by deposit size and tenure:

weight = sqrt(depositAmount_wei) × floor_log2(months_as_member + 1)
  • sqrt component — rewards larger deposits but with diminishing returns. Adding to your deposit before a vote closes increases your weight immediately.
  • log2 component — rewards longer membership. Zero for the first 30 days, preventing flash-deposit governance attacks.
  • Dynamic finalization — Weights are recomputed at finalization time. A voter who deposited more after casting their vote will have their increased weight counted in the final tally.
  • The large numbers (e.g. 1,000,000,000) are normal — the formula operates on raw wei values.

Governance

The treasurer proposes parameter changes; members vote on them using the same weighted formula as loan votes.

  1. Treasurer submits a proposal on the Treasurer tab.
  2. Members vote Yes / No on the Governance tab within the 3-day window. The same dynamic-weight finalization applies — weights are counted at finalization, not at cast time.
  3. Approved proposals enter a 2-day timelock before anyone can execute them.
  4. The treasurer can veto a pending proposal before the voting window closes.

Parameters that can be changed: Max Loan Size, Min Interest Rate, Keeper Bounty Rate, Unpause, Transfer Treasurer.

Dev Tools

Available on the Pool Overview tab. Uses Hardhat's evm_increaseTime RPC call to move the chain clock forward without waiting in real time. This is essential for local testing because the contract enforces real time-based rules (30-day voting tenure, 3-day vote windows, repayment deadlines).

  • Advance 31 days after joining so members accumulate voting weight.
  • Advance 3 days after a loan request to close the voting window and enable Finalize.
  • Advance past the loan's repayment deadline to test Trigger Default.
